The copyable title, descriptions and release note are in `metadata/en-US/`. The title is limited to 30 characters, the short description to 80, and the full description to 4,000. The asset-validation script checks these limits and image dimensions.

Upload the eight `images/phoneScreenshots/*.png` in numeric order. They use real app screens and contain no real banking information. Captures showing source apps reflect the emulator; installed sources on a customer's phone will vary. There is no promotional video; video is optional for this phone-app listing. Do not upload unrelated tablet, Wear, TV, XR or Automotive images.

Select only relevant tags actually offered in Play Console. Do not add “bank”, a bank's logo, ranking claims, fake endorsements or “official” bank affiliation to the listing. The app is an assistant companion, not a banking service.

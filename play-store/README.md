# Legacy Link — Google Play submission kit

Developer: Legacy Studios · Support: legacystudiosofficial@gmail.com

App ID: `mv.legacy.link` · Version: `2.1.0` · Version code: `6` · Android: 8.0+ · Target API: 36

## Start here

1. Use `builds/Legacy-Link-2.1.0-play.aab` for a new Play Console release. It is signed with a private upload key held locally outside the repository. Keep that key and its credentials backed up privately.
2. Paste the English listing from `metadata/en-US/`. Upload `icon.png`, `featureGraphic.png` and the eight ordered `phoneScreenshots` from its `images/` folder.
3. Use the public privacy, support and deletion URLs below. Confirm each opens without login before submitting.
4. Complete App content using `documentation/APP_CONTENT.md`, `DATA_SAFETY.md`, and `APP_ACCESS.md`.
5. Provide a working, reviewer-accessible HTTPS assistant and private test pairing credentials in Play Console. Do not publish those credentials in this repository.
6. Upload to internal testing, verify the complete link on physical devices, then meet any testing requirements attached to your developer account before applying for production access.

## Owner actions before submission

The developer-account identity, address/verification, distribution countries, pricing/tax settings, actual assistant hosting and downstream AI-provider practices, private reviewer credentials, IARC rating, and any required closed testing must be completed or confirmed in your own Play Console account. They cannot be inferred from the app source. This kit does not submit to Google Play or claim approval. The Data safety document distinguishes verified app behavior from receiver practices that you must confirm.

The personal APK permits private-network HTTP and is not the Google Play upload. Use the file labeled `play.aab` for Play.

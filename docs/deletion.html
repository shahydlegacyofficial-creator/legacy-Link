Legacy Link data deletion

Legacy Link has no Legacy Studios account registration. Most app data is stored on your phone; notifications that you send are held by the assistant server you selected.

Remove app-local data

  1. Open Legacy Link → Settings → Clear local data.
  2. Read the confirmation and select Clear local data.
  3. The saved address and secret, app selections, consent, waiting notifications, connection result, and last-delivery time are removed. Forwarding is paused.

You can also use Android Settings → Apps → Legacy Link → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Names vary by device. This removes app-local information, not copies already sent to an assistant or notifications belonging to another app.

Remove data from your assistant

Ask the operator of the assistant endpoint you configured to remove received notification events, server logs, backups and any copies passed to connected AI services. Legacy Link does not have remote deletion authority over independent receivers. If you operate the assistant, use that system's data controls and retention settings.

Information sent to Legacy Studios support

Email legacystudiosofficial@gmail.com with the subject “Legacy Link privacy request” to ask about information you sent to support. Identify the email address used to contact us and describe the request. Do not include bank credentials, notification contents, one-time codes or pairing secrets. We will assess requests under applicable law and may need to verify that the correspondence belongs to you.
